Amendment to the OSF protocol (Pass-3 protocol v1.2): secondary analysis of the full set
=======================================================================================

Survey of published 1H NMR descriptions (Organic Letters, first half of 2026)
Author: Artur V. Eshtukov-Shcheglov
Date written: 18 October 2026

This file was written and published before any summary of the tool's results for the full
set of descriptions (the share of flagged descriptions, overall or per checklist item) was
computed or viewed. It will be attached to the formal OSF amendment.


1. What has been done so far
----------------------------
The tool (extract_names, names_to_smiles, check_descriptions; frozen version of
18 October 2026; SHA-256 below) was run on all 201 SI
files (200 articles; one article has two SI files) of the Org. Lett. sample. It found
8,609 1H NMR descriptions. The run was reproduced on a second computer: the
check_descriptions tables were identical, and the extract_names tables were identical for
199 of 201 files (one file differed only in whitespace after conversion from .docx; one
file had been converted from the wrong .docx and was replaced and rerun).
Only the numbers of descriptions, of structures and of descriptions marked by the tool as
possibly known or repeated (KNOWN? 686, REPEAT 248) were looked at; no result of the checks
(check_descriptions) was summarised.

2. Primary analysis (unchanged)
-------------------------------
The primary result of the survey remains the assessment of the 982 randomly selected
descriptions of new compounds (5 per article, or all if n < 5) by the expert reviewer
according to protocol v1.2 and the application decisions recorded in the amendment.


3. Secondary analysis of the full set (added)
---------------------------------------------
(a) Population. All 1H NMR descriptions found by the tool in the 200 SI files, without
    selecting new compounds (descriptions of known compounds and repeated descriptions
    are included).
(b) Accuracy of the tool. The tool's agreement with the expert assessment is measured on
    the 982 reference descriptions, per checklist item (items 1, 3, 4(a) and 6):
    sensitivity, specificity, and the confusion table.
(c) Full-set estimate. For the full set, the share of descriptions flagged by the tool is
    reported overall and per checklist item, together with an estimate corrected for the
    sensitivity and specificity measured in (b), with 95% confidence intervals that account
    for clustering by article.
(d) Transfer check. Because accuracy is measured on new compounds only, flag rates are
    compared between descriptions marked by the tool as possibly known (KNOWN?) and
    unmarked descriptions. A large difference is reported as a limitation of the
    full-set estimate.
(e) Item 1 is checked only for descriptions for which a structure was obtained from the
    compound name; the share of such descriptions is reported, and the item-1 estimate is
    stated to apply to this subset only.
(f) Items 2, 4(b) and 7 are not checked by the tool and are not estimated for the full set.
(g) The secondary analysis does not replace or modify the primary analysis; where the two
    disagree, the primary analysis is reported as the main result.
