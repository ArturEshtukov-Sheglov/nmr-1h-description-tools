OSF REGISTRATION doi:10.17605/OSF.IO/EQ9AH — UPDATE 9 (5 November 2026)

1. Status of the assessment
Unchanged since Update 5: 307 of the 982 selected Organic Letters descriptions reviewed. The version of the tools
used for the survey (frozen 17.10.2026, v1.1) is unchanged. The blind test (Update 8) is not affected.

2. checkHNMR V2.1.4-beta (5 November 2026)
Two new checks in the check scripts, both for descriptions with a structure:
- Item 4(b) (check_descriptions/item4b.py): the proton next to a CH3 doublet. A CH3 reported as a doublet shows that
  its coupling to the neighbouring CH is resolved, so that CH couples to three equivalent protons with this constant
  and must show at least a quartet for it. If every signal that reports this constant shows fewer than three
  partners for it ("d", "dd", "t") and no multiplet without constants, broad or apparent signal could be that CH,
  item 4(b) is "candidate (check)". Applied when the structure has a CH3 bonded to a carbon with one H (not next to
  Si); CH3 doublet of 3, 6 or 9 H below 4.5 ppm with one constant of 5.0-8.5 Hz; the CH is looked for among signals
  of 1 H (or n/3 H) up to 6.5 ppm (7.6 ppm if the CH can be =CH-CH3), with the tolerance of item 3.
- Item 6(a) (check_descriptions/structure_checks.py, verdicts.py, excel_output.py): CH2-CH2 of an open chain
  (X-CH2-CH2-Y, including the inner CH2 of alkyl chains) written with a first-order multiplicity and a coupling
  constant is reported as the subcategory "flexible chain" of protocol v1.2, item 6(a) (formally AA'XX', usually
  deceptively simple), not as an inconsistency, when the structure has no saturated-ring CH2-CH2. This implements
  a subcategory of the protocol that earlier versions did not report (listed as not checked in Update 3 and in the
  paper on checkHNMR). The conclusion of a description does not change.
Origin of the item-4(b) rule: the idea came from a description seen while preparing the cards of the blind test
(Update 8), an Organic Letters description of the survey sample. That description was not used to build or test the
rule and is left out of any accuracy measurement of V2.1.x. The rule was built on synthetic descriptions and checked
on development data only. Protocol v1.2 is not changed: its item 4(c) does not count a multiplicity with fewer
partners than the structure gives (a small constant may stay unresolved); the survey reference keeps applying
v1.2, and this case will be described as a limitation of the protocol in the survey paper.
Development data used: 5,120 descriptions with structures from 300 J. Org. Chem. 2024-2025 articles (already
development data, PMCID list in the checkHNMR repository). Compared with V2.1.3-beta: 3 descriptions get the new
item-4(b) finding (all real errors of the description: a CH next to a CH3 written as "ddd" with a 12.5 Hz constant,
and two isopropyl CH septets written as "dt" or "dd" with twice the constant); 260 get the item-6(a) subcategory;
no other verdict and no conclusion changed. A first version of the item-4(b) rule also matched an aromatic triplet
with a similar constant; the shift limit for the CH was added before the release.
Tests: tests/test_v214_fixes.py (10 tests, synthetic descriptions only; 100 unit tests in all). Self-test with
100,000 synthetic descriptions per test (seed 20261023): all pass; a new error in the structure stress test
("ch_methyl": the CH next to a CH3 doublet written as a doublet with the CH3 constant) is found in 93.4 % of
cases, with no false findings on correct descriptions (summary: docs/selftest_V2.1.4-beta_100k.txt in the checkHNMR
repository). The reading of descriptions (description_parser.py) did not change: realistic reading test (100,000
descriptions, seed 260626): 39 misread (0.04 %), as V2.1.3-beta.
Code: GitHub checkHNMR, commit c3f25c1; Zenodo version doi:10.5281/zenodo.23966635 (all versions:
doi:10.5281/zenodo.23749627). SHA-256 of all scripts: preregistration/SHA256_V2.1.4-beta_2026-11-05.txt
(commit 507eb52; SHA-256 of the file 01b5282f8a5e36199a2ac4eac926264b899bead6748b969a92001fc6a1656dac).
Compared with V2.1.3-beta, check_descriptions/item4b.py, structure_checks.py, verdicts.py, excel_output.py,
run_all.py and tests/stress/structure_test.py changed and tests/test_v214_fixes.py was added.

3. Website checkhnmr.org
5 November 2026: the website runs V2.1.4-beta (same check scripts as the local version). The item-4(b) finding is
shown as its own alert B ("Item 4(b). Proton next to a CH3 doublet is not a quartet"); the item-6(a) subcategory as
an alert C ("Item 6(a). Open-chain CH2–CH2 in first-order notation"). Website (Pyodide with RDKit.js) and Python
gave identical verification codes, verdicts and alerts on the 5,120 development descriptions with structures.
Version V2.1.3-beta, described in the paper on checkHNMR, is kept unchanged at checkhnmr.org/v2.1.3-beta/.

4. Use in the survey
None of these changes affects the survey: its results are obtained with the frozen version v1.1. The accuracy of
V2.1.x is to be measured only on test articles not used in its development (Update 3).
