This update records what has been decided or added since Update 1. It does not change the

--------------------------------

  18-21 October 2026). The review of the remaining 675 is in progress.
- Blind test, external rater, comparison models (GPT, Qwen, DeepSeek): unchanged since

- Second data set (Update 1, section 7): not yet registered; no article of it has been opened.


2. Review procedure (clarification of Update 1, section 1)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Update 1 describes the reference as "the author's review of every first-pass decision". The
procedure, used for all reviewed descriptions from the start of the review, is: the author
reads the description and the structure and makes his own assessment of each checklist item;
only then does he read the first-pass decision and its reasons, and records agreement or
disagreement with it and, where they differ, his own decision. The reference is the author's
decision.


3. Decisions on the open questions of Update 1, section 4(e)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Both were taken on 21 October 2026, during the review of the first batch, from descriptions
in it, and before the reference is finalised. Results will be reported under these
decisions, and the number of descriptions affected under the alternative will be given.

(1) Item 7 and the borderline zone of item 6(b). A first-order-looking multiplet (e.g. an
    aromatic "t") of a proton whose partner pair lies in the borderline strong-coupling zone
    (6 <= Δν/J < 8) is not assessed under item 7; it is recorded in the item-6 subcategory
    "borderline strong coupling". Such multiplets outside the borderline zone remain under
    item 7.
(2) Item 4(c). The exception "fewer partners than expected is not counted" does not apply to
    a coupling that cannot remain unresolved when the coupling to the same proton that is
    reported is resolved. Omitting such a coupling is an item-4 inconsistency. This covers
    large heteronuclear couplings (2J(H,F) of about 45-50 Hz, 1J(P,H), large 2J(P,H)) and,
    as decided later the same day after the second batch, large H-H couplings: aromatic ortho
    (about 7-9 Hz), vicinal (about 7 Hz) in CH3-CH2, CH-CH3 and CH2-CH2 fragments, geminal
    (about 12-16 Hz) of separately reported CH2 protons, and vinyl cis/trans couplings. Small
    couplings (meta, other 4J, small H-F couplings) remain under 4(c) and are not counted.
    The extended rule will be applied to all 982 descriptions, including those already
    reviewed, before the reference is finalised.


4. Further application decisions (protocol v1.2 unchanged)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Taken on 21 October 2026, after the second batch had been reviewed; they apply to all 982
descriptions.

(a) A coupling constant that is implausible in magnitude for the proton to which it can belong
    (e.g. a pyridine H-5 reported as a doublet with J = 10.7 Hz) is given a note "suspicious
    J by magnitude". The item-3 verdict follows the existing rule (partner not verifiable) and
    is not changed by the note. The number of descriptions with the note will be reported.
(b) Formally diastereotopic protons very far from the stereocentre (e.g. a CH2 more than ten
    bonds away) are given a note that they may be isochronous; the item-7 verdict follows the
    formal reading.
(c) When the verdict depends on an assignment that cannot be established from the published
    data, the reading in the authors' favour is taken (the description is consistent if there
    is an assignment under which it is consistent).


5. Tool versions (addition to Update 1, section 2)
--------------------------------------------------
(a) The results of the paper remain those of the frozen version (Update 1, section 2(a)).
(b) Version 2 (V2, 22-23 October 2026) changes results: extraction fixes (Symbol-font
    characters, isomer descriptions, ion types such as [M-N2+H]+ and [M-H+]-, isotopologue
    masses, compound labels), a partial check of item 4(b) (an upper bound without an
    assignment), and an item-3 finding for coupling constants above 25 Hz in compounds that
    contain no F, P or other spin-active heteronucleus. V2 is not used for any result of the
    Organic Letters set.
(c) Data used to develop V2: the supporting information of six articles outside the sample
    (Org. Lett. 2023 and 2014, ACS Catal. 2015 and three J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2026 articles);
    the 201 SI files of the Organic Letters sample (regression checks); the compound labels
    of the 982 reference descriptions (to check label changes); and the 982 reference
    descriptions with their structures (to tune the item-4(b) check). V2 figures on these data
    are reported for information only and are not measures of accuracy. The accuracy of V2
    will be measured only on the test articles of the second data set, under the update that
    registers that set (Update 1, section 7).
(d) The V2 code will be published with the paper. Its SHA-256 values are deposited now:
    preregistration/SHA256_V2_2026-10-23.txt
    (SHA-256 9a4b3800441e677a407263f5b1975c70f5a516b0937ef57efe9c0a6ed3e5e65f),
    commit b2d9ba0 (23 October 2026).
(e) Planned version 1.1.1: an interactive prompt to install missing Python packages. It is
    intended to change no result; equivalence will be checked as for version 1.1 (all output
    files of the 201 SI identical) before it is used.
(f) An auxiliary script that converts the review workbook into a Word document (one compound
    per page) is used for reading and presenting the review. It only reads the workbook and
    changes no decision.
(g) A public web version of the tool, running V2 (marked as a beta until its accuracy has been
    measured), is planned. Every report it produces states the version and date; uploaded data
    are not stored. It is not used for any result of the paper.
