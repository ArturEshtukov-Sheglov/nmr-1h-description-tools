Update 10 (17 November 2026): checkHNMR V2.1.5-beta

1. New version of checkHNMR

checkHNMR V2.1.5-beta was released on 17 November 2026: GitHub tag v2.1.5-beta (commit cdb2b46), Zenodo https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24314643 (all versions: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23749627). Update 9 described the previous version, V2.1.4-beta. The commit after the tag (7f2ae85) changes only the Zenodo metadata file .zenodo.json, which is not in the hash list. The changes are described in CHANGELOG.md and docs/DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md of the repository. In short:
(a) item 4(b): counting of large couplings (J ≥ 6 Hz) with a multiplet-width test, and the range of each coupling constant by the type of the proton pair;
(b) item 1: the integrals must fit the groups of equivalent protons;
(c) a ¹⁹F or ³¹P description that gives only shifts is no longer read as singlets; the H–X partner of a constant without a ¹H partner is unverifiable;
(d) a description with no coupling constant and at least one "m" is reported as not checked by items 3, 4(a) and 6, instead of "no inconsistency found".

3. Website

The website runs V2.1.5-beta. Its code gave the same verdicts, findings, alerts and verification codes in the browser (Pyodide with RDKit.js) as in Python with RDKit on the 5,136 development descriptions from J. Org. Chem. that have structures. V2.1.4-beta is kept at https://checkhnmr.org/v2.1.4-beta/ with its Python, scripts and libraries unchanged, so the verification codes of reports made with it can still be reproduced. The archived V2.1.3-beta at https://checkhnmr.org/v2.1.3-beta/ is unchanged: its files are byte-identical to those before this update.

4. Development data and the survey

The Supporting Information of ol5c05448 (Org. Lett. 2026), an article of the survey sample, was used as development data of V2.1.5 after its assessment in the survey (docs/DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md). It stays in the survey. The results of V2.1.5 on this article are development results and are not used to measure accuracy. The development articles that are not in the PMCID lists of the repository, ol5c05448 included, are now listed in docs/development_data/DEVELOPMENT_DATA_OTHER.txt (commit 3c29a23, section 5). The checks of the survey were made with versions before V2.0; this update does not change them. If V2.1.5 is used in the survey, I will register that use in a separate update before the run, and the articles used to develop V2.1.5 will be excluded from that analysis or reported separately.

5. Corrections to earlier updates

The same errors in the repository texts (docs/DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md, VERSION, README.md) were corrected after the release, in commit 3c29a23 on the main branch. That commit changes text only; no file in list (a) changed.

(a) Update 3, 3(b), says that the Organic Letters data were used in V2.1 for regression checks and for comparison. They were also used to calibrate the reading-quality marker of V2.1 on the 8,609 descriptions of the 201 SI files of the sample (24 October 2026), and changed findings among the 982 selected descriptions were judged against the survey reference (25–26 October 2026; docs/DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md, CHANGELOG.md).

(b) Update 3, 3(c) and 3(d), say that Organic Letters articles of any year were never used as development data and that no article published in 2026 was used for development. Both statements are wrong for V2 and every later version, which inherit its code. As Update 2, 5(c), states, V2 was developed in part on the Supporting Information of two Organic Letters articles (ol3c04097, 2023; ol503595m, 2014) and three J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2026 articles (ja5c20554, ja6c04560, ja6c10875), and the upper bound of item 4(b) was tuned on the 982 selected descriptions of the survey. V2.1.5 adds ol5c05448 (section 4). All of these are development data, listed in docs/development_data/DEVELOPMENT_DATA_OTHER.txt; the accuracy of a version is measured only on data that are not development data (Update 1, 2(c)).

(c) Update 9 refers to a case "listed as not checked in Update 3". Update 3 contains no list of cases that are not checked. The only such list in the updates is in Update 7 (items 2, 5 and 7), and it does not include item 6(a).

(d) The version that gives the results of the survey. Update 4 (2(c)) and Updates 6, 7 and 9 (and, until commit 3c29a23, VERSION and README.md of checkHNMR) call it "frozen 17.10.2026, v1.1" or "frozen 17.10c / v1.1". As Update 1 (2(a)–(b)) states, the results of the survey are those of the frozen version of 18 October 2026, whose hashes are given in the amendment of 18 October 2026: the measuring scripts of 17 October 2026 (version 17c; count_1H_descriptions of 17d) with run_all.py of 18 October; hence the two dates. Version 1.1 is a code cleanup made after the freeze: on all 201 SI files of the sample its output tables are identical to those of the frozen version (EQUIVALENCE_v1.1_2026-10-18.txt), but it is not the frozen version. In Updates 1–4, "the paper" is the paper of this survey; in Update 9, it is the article on checkHNMR V2.1.3-beta.
