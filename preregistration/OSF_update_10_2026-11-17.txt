OSF REGISTRATION doi:10.17605/OSF.IO/EQ9AH — UPDATE 10 (17 November 2026)

1. Status of the assessment
Unchanged since Update 5: 307 of the 982 selected Organic Letters descriptions reviewed. The version of the tools
used for the survey is unchanged: the frozen version of 18 October 2026 (Update 1, section 2(a); on its label in
earlier updates, see section 5(d)). The blind test (Update 8) is not affected.

2. checkHNMR V2.1.5-beta (17 November 2026)
Changes in the check scripts (the effect of each rule on the development data is given in CHANGELOG.md of the
checkHNMR repository):
- Item 4(b) (check_descriptions/width_count.py, new): counting of large couplings (J ≥ 6 Hz) with a multiplet-width
  test, and the range of each coupling constant by the type of the proton pair.
- Item 1: the integrals must fit the groups of equivalent protons.
- Item 3: a ¹⁹F or ³¹P description that gives only shifts is no longer read as singlets; the H–X partner of a
  constant without a ¹H partner is unverifiable.
- A description with no coupling constant and at least one "m" is reported as not checked by items 3, 4(a) and 6,
  instead of "no inconsistency found".
The width test is justified in the note "checkHNMR V2.1.5: Justification of the multiplet-width test" (17 November
2026, 15 pages), which gives the source of each numerical bound. The note is deposited with this update as
checkHNMR_V2.1.5_width_test.pdf (SHA-256 cc793e19a38902e3ddf310de694a549c930d8d3c89670e48d519e892823fb227,
423,065 bytes); the same file is in the checkHNMR repository (docs/development/width_criterion/, with the code that
reproduces its computed numbers) and on the website.
Development data used: 5,136 descriptions with structures from 300 J. Org. Chem. 2024-2025 articles (8,540 rows;
already development data, PMCID list in the checkHNMR repository) and the Supporting Information of ol5c05448
(section 4). Compared with V2.1.4-beta on the J. Org. Chem. data, the conclusions changed as follows: inconsistent
under items 1-4, 1,229 -> 1,268; under item 6 only, 1,239 -> 1,221; to check, 4,312 -> 4,297; no finding,
1,760 -> 1,125; not checked (new), 629.
Tests: tests/test_v215_fixes.py (13 tests, synthetic descriptions only; 113 unit tests in all). Self-test with
100,000 synthetic descriptions per test (seed 20261023): all pass, with no false findings of the new rules on
correct descriptions (summary: docs/selftest_V2.1.5-beta_100k.txt in the checkHNMR repository). Realistic reading
test (100,000 descriptions, seed 260626): 39 misread (0.04 %), as V2.1.4-beta.
Code: GitHub checkHNMR, tag v2.1.5-beta (commit cdb2b46); Zenodo version doi:10.5281/zenodo.24314643 (all versions:
doi:10.5281/zenodo.23749627). The commit after the tag (7f2ae85) changes only the Zenodo metadata file

4. Use in the survey
None of these changes affects the survey: its results are obtained with the frozen version (Update 1, section
2(a)). As registered in Update 1, section 2(c), V2.1.5 is not used for the results of the Organic Letters set, and
its accuracy will be measured only on data not used to develop it, under an update registered before that
measurement. The Supporting Information of ol5c05448 (Org. Lett. 2026), an article of the survey sample, was used
as development data of V2.1.5 after its assessment in the survey (docs/DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md and
docs/development_data/DEVELOPMENT_DATA_OTHER.txt of the checkHNMR repository). It stays in the survey; the results
of V2.1.5 on it are development results, not a measure of accuracy. Any other use of V2.1.5 in the survey will be
registered in an update before it, with the articles used to develop V2.1.5 excluded or reported separately.

5. Corrections to earlier updates
The same errors in the texts of the checkHNMR repository (docs/DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md, VERSION, README.md) were
corrected after the release, in commit 3c29a23 on its main branch (text only; no file of the SHA-256 list changed).
(a) Update 3, section 3(b), says that, as for V2, the 201 SI files of the Organic Letters sample were used for
regression checks and the 982 reference descriptions for comparison. In V2.1 these data were also used to calibrate
the reading-quality marker, on the 8,609 descriptions of the 201 SI files (24 October 2026), and changed findings
among the 982 reference descriptions were judged against the reference (25-26 October 2026; DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md and
CHANGELOG.md of the checkHNMR repository).
(b) Update 3, sections 3(c) and 3(d), say that the journals of the survey, Org. Lett. included (any year), are
never used as development data, and that no article published in 2026 was used for development. Both statements
are wrong for V2 and every later version, which inherit its code: as Update 2, section 5(c), states, V2 was
developed in part on the supporting information of two Org. Lett. articles (2023 and 2014) and three J. Am. Chem.
Soc. 2026 articles, and its item-4(b) check was tuned on the 982 reference descriptions with their structures.
These data and ol5c05448 (section 4) are listed in docs/development_data/DEVELOPMENT_DATA_OTHER.txt of the checkHNMR
repository; as Update 1, section 2(c), requires, none of them is used to measure accuracy.
(c) Update 9, section 2, says that the subcategory "flexible chain" of item 6(a) was "listed as not checked in
Update 3 and in the paper on checkHNMR". Update 3 has no list of what is not checked and does not mention this
subcategory; the reference to Update 3 is wrong.
(d) Update 4 (section 3(c): "The frozen version used in the paper (version 1.1, SHA256_v1.1_2026-10-18.txt)") and
Updates 6, 7 and 9 (section 1: "frozen 17.10.2026, v1.1"; last section: "the frozen version v1.1") identify the
frozen version with version 1.1. These are two versions (Update 1, section 2). The results of the survey are those
of the frozen version of 18 October 2026, whose SHA-256 values are in OSF_amendment_fullset_2026-10-18.txt: the
measuring scripts of 17 October 2026 (version 17c, with count_1H_descriptions of 17d) and run_all.py of 18 October,
hence the two dates. Version 1.1 (Update 1, section 2(b)) is a later reorganization of the code whose output files
on all 201 SI files are identical to those of the frozen version (EQUIVALENCE_v1.1_2026-10-18.txt); it is not the
version that gives the results.
